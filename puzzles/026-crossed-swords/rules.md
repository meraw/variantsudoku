# Crossed Swords — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**German whispers (green blades):** neighbouring digits along a green line differ by at least 5.

**Dutch whispers (orange hilts):** neighbouring digits along an orange line differ by at least 4.

**Battlefield:** in a row or column with a clue, the first digit is the size of an army standing at that end, and the last
digit is the size of an army standing at the other end. The clue is the sum of the digits in the cells where the two armies
overlap. If the armies don't meet, it is the sum of the digits in the gap between them instead (0 if they just touch). The
clues are written on the top and left only, but they read the same from either end.
