# Product Placement — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**Bridges (invented rule, teal lines):** each line connects two circled end cells. The **product** of the two circled
digits equals the **sum** of all the digits on the line between them. Digits may repeat on a line if sudoku rules allow.

*Examples:* a three-cell bridge 2 – 6 – 3 works (2 × 3 = 6). A four-cell bridge 2 – 1 – 5 – 3 works (2 × 3 = 1 + 5).

Given digits: 2.
