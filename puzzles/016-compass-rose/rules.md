# Compass Rose — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**Arrows:** the digits along an arrow sum to the digit in its circle. All eight arrows start from the same central circle, and each
arrow on its own sums to that circle.

**Sandwich sums:** a number outside the grid gives the sum of the digits between the 1 and the 9 in that row or column.

Play: see [play.md](play.md).
