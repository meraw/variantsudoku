# Counting Stars — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**Counting circles:** a digit in a circle equals the number of circles in the grid that contain that digit. So a circled 3
means that exactly three circles hold a 3. All 15 circles count, including the ones on lines.

**Between lines (grey lines joining circles):** every digit on a line lies strictly between the digits in the two circles at
its ends. The middle star of the belt (r5c5) is an ordinary cell of the belt's line, so it lies between r6c4 and r4c6.

**Sandwich sums:** a number outside the grid is the sum of the digits between the 1 and the 9 in that row or column.

No given digits.
