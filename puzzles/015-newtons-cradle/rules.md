# Newton's Cradle — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**Pendulums (invented rule, slate lines):** each slate line hangs from a circle. The circled digit is the pendulum's *resting point*.
Read the line away from the circle. The digits swing **alternately above and below** the circled digit (never equal to it), and every
swing is **strictly smaller** than the one before: each digit is closer to the circled digit than the previous digit was.
The first swing may go up or down.

*Example:* circle 5, then 9, 2, 6 is a pendulum: swings of 4 up, 3 down, 1 up. Circle 5, then 9, 2, 8 is not (the third swing, 3, is not
smaller than the second, 3), and neither is 5, 9, 7 (two swings up in a row).
