# Bowstring — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**Double arrow (the brown bow):** the digits in the two circles at the bow's ends add up to the sum of the digits on the
line between them.

**Between line (the grey bowstring):** it shares the bow's two end circles. Every digit on the string between the
circles lies strictly between the two circled digits.

**Arrow (the dark arrow nocked on the string):** the digit in its circle equals the sum of the digits along its shaft.

**X-sums:** a number outside the grid is the sum of the first X digits of that row or column, read from the clue's
side, where X is the first of those digits.

No given digits.
