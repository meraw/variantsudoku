# Flat-Pack City — rules

**Jigsaw sudoku:** place 1–9 once each in every row, every column and every **bold-outlined region**. The regions are irregular: each one is
a 3×3 box with jigsaw-puzzle tabs and slots.

**Skyscrapers:** each digit is the height of a building. A number outside the grid gives how many buildings you can see from that side,
looking along that row or column. A building hides every shorter building behind it. *Example:* seen from the left, the row
3 1 5 2 9 … shows 3, 5 and 9, so that is three buildings.

Play: see [play.md](play.md).
