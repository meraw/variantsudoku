# Happy Medium — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**German whispers (green lines):** neighbouring digits along a green line differ by at least 5. The outline of the face is
one closed loop, so its first and last cells (r1c3 and r2c2) are neighbours on the line too.

**Median:** a clue outside the grid is the median (the middle value) of the first three digits in that row or column, read
from the clue. So of those three digits, one is smaller than the clue, one equals it and one is larger. (Median clues as in
clover's Median Sudoku, Genuinely Approachable Sudoku series, GM Puzzles 2026.)
