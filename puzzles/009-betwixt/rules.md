# Betwixt — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**Sandwich sums:** a number outside the grid gives the sum of the digits that lie *between* the 1 and the 9 in that
row or column (in whichever order the 1 and 9 appear). A sum of 0 would mean the 1 and 9 are adjacent.

**Between lines (grey lines with circles at both ends):** every digit on the line, apart from the two circled ends,
is strictly greater than the smaller circled digit and strictly less than the larger one.

No given digits.
