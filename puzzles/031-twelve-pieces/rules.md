# Twelve Pieces — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**Killer cages:** digits in a cage do not repeat and add up to the small number in the cage's top-left corner.
The twelve cages are the twelve **pentominoes** — F, I, L, N, P, T, U, V, W, X, Y and Z — each used exactly once
(turned or flipped). The pale colours only help you tell the pieces apart; they carry no extra rule.

**Anti-king:** two cells that touch diagonally may not contain the same digit.

No given digits.
