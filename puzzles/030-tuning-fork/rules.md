# Tuning Fork — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**Fifths lines (blue, invented):** digits next to each other along a blue line differ by 4 or 5. Digits may repeat on a
line where sudoku allows. The fork's U (from r1c3 down, round the bottom and up to r1c7) is one line, and the handle
(r6c5 down to r9c5) is a second line that starts on the U's middle cell.

**Killer cages:** digits in a cage do not repeat and add up to the small number in the cage's top-left corner.

No given digits.

Play: see [play.md](play.md).
