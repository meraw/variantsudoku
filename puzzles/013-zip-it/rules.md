# Zip It — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**Zipper lines (purple):** digits an equal distance from the centre of a purple line sum to the same total. On a line with an
odd number of cells (every zipper here has 3 or 5 cells), that total is the digit in the centre cell.
*Example:* a five-cell line 2 – 6 – 9 – 3 – 7 works: 2 + 7 = 6 + 3 = 9, the centre.

**Dutch whispers (orange):** neighbouring digits along an orange line differ by at least 4.

Lines may cross. Each line keeps its own rule.

One given digit.
