# Leapfrog — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**Leapfrog clues (invented):** a frog sits on each green lily pad outside the grid. It jumps onto the first cell of that row or column. Then it
keeps hopping forward, away from its pad, by as many cells as the digit it is standing on, until it hops out of the grid. The number on the
pad is the **sum of the digits the frog lands on**.

Example: a row reading 2 7 5 1 9 3 8 6 4 from the pad. The frog lands on the 2, hops 2 cells to the 5, hops 5 cells to the 6, then hops 6
cells and leaves the grid. The clue would be 2 + 5 + 6 = 13.
