# Trail Map — rules

Normal sudoku rules apply: place 1–9 once each in every row, column and 3×3 box.

**Summits (complete map):** a cell is a *summit* if its digit is larger than every orthogonally adjacent digit (the cells
directly left, right, above and below it). Every summit in the grid is shaded and marked with a small mountain, **and no other
cell is a summit**: every unmarked cell has at least one orthogonal neighbour with a larger digit.

**Thermometers (trails):** digits strictly increase along each grey thermometer, starting from the bulb.
